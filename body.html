Introducing Nexus Netra

A clearer view of your business.
A stronger foundation for decisions.

As businesses adopt technology and AI, leaders need information they can understand, question and act on. Performance reports, control findings and risk registers become more useful when they tell a connected story.

Nexus Netra is ARC’s Governance Operating System, bringing business performance, control monitoring and enterprise risk into a common governance framework.

The purpose is straightforward: help teams

  1. See what needs attention
  2. Understand the underlying issue
  3. Follow through with clear accountability

The meaning of Netra

NETRA नेत्र

Netra means eye. It represents our belief that better governance begins with the ability to see clearly.

The eye that sees. The vision that guides.

  • PerformanceA performance shortfall

    should prompt a closer look at its causes.

  • ControlsA recurring control exception

    should inform the assessment of risk.

  • RiskA mitigation plan

    should have an owner, a timeline and a way to track progress.

Nexus Netra is being developed around these connections, combining ARC’s domain knowledge with technology and AI to make governance more timely and useful.

Technology with business understanding

AI that helps people get to the issue faster.

Our approach to AI starts with the work people need to do: understand a variance, review an exception, identify a pattern or prepare a management discussion.

Across the Nexus Netra roadmap, AI is intended to support plain-language commentary, anomaly identification, document analysis and easier access to information. Each capability is introduced around the needs and data available within the agreed solution.

Business context and professional judgment remain central.ARC’s role is to help teams interpret what the information means and decide what action is appropriate.
